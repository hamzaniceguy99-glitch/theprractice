How we work

Repeat, film, correct

Consistency is the whole sport. An anchor two millimeters off, a grip that tightens, a release that collapses — each one opens the group, and none of them feel like anything from inside the shot.

Video resolves it. Side-on footage at normal speed, slowed down, shows alignment and follow-through clearly, and the correction is usually one specific thing rather than a list.

Then it's repetition with intent. Blank bale work, structured sessions and honest scoring move archers forward far faster than shooting hundreds of arrows at a target face.
