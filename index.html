How we work

Online coaching, range rules are yours

Nobody from The Practice is standing beside you. We coach from video, which works remarkably well for form, and we sell and inspect no equipment.

Archery equipment is dangerous when it's misused or damaged. Shoot only at a proper range or with a safe backstop, follow the range's rules and whistle commands, never dry fire a bow, and have draw weight, draw length and arrow spine checked by a qualified technician before you change anything significant.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Can archery form really be coached by video?

Yes, unusually well. Form is visible from the side, and slow-motion review catches collapses and alignment issues the archer can't feel.

Recurve, compound or barebow?

All three are coached here. The shot sequence principles overlap, and the tuning and release work differ — tell us what you shoot.

What draw weight should I use?

Lower than most beginners choose. If you can't hold it comfortably at full draw for ten seconds, your form will never settle.

Do I need a club?

A safe range matters more. Clubs are excellent for range access and in-person safety instruction, and we'd encourage one.

My groups are good then suddenly terrible.

Usually the sequence breaking down rather than aim. Video over several ends shows exactly where it goes.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.

Want your form looked at properly?

Six arrows on video, twenty minutes of feedback. Free.
