The programs, in detail

Four programs of 8 to 12 weeks, built on video of your own shooting.

Program 01 · Built from the ground up

Form & alignment

We rebuild form from the feet up: stance width and angle, posture without leaning, a relaxed bow hand, and the shoulder alignment that lets your back do the work. Blank bale shooting with no target removes the temptation to aim and lets form settle.

  • 10 weeks
  • Beginners up
  • 3 sessions a week
  • A bow and a safe range

Start this program

Form without a target to chase.

The sequence surviving a scored round.

Program 02 · Same every arrow

The shot sequence

A shot sequence is a list you run identically whether it's practice or the last arrow of a round. We build yours, work on a consistent anchor, expansion through the clicker or the wall, a release that doesn't collapse, and follow-through that proves nothing stopped early.

  • 10 weeks
  • Form basics done
  • 3 sessions a week
  • Your own bow

Start this program

Program 03 · Fitted to you

Equipment & tuning

Plenty of form problems are equipment problems. We check draw length and weight against what you can hold, match arrow spine, and work through basic tuning and bare shaft testing. Anything structural goes to a qualified technician — and we'll say when it should.

  • 8 weeks
  • Own equipment
  • 2 sessions a week
  • Basic tools

Program 04 · Scores that move

Practice & competition

Shooting three hundred arrows without a plan mostly practices your current mistakes. We build sessions with a purpose, score regularly to track real progress, and prepare for competition — including the routine and breathing that keep the shot sequence intact when it counts.

  • 8 weeks
  • Shooting regularly
  • 3 sessions a week
  • A scorecard
